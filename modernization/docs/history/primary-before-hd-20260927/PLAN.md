# Modernization implementation

## Requirements

Target 30 logic updates per second plus 60 distinct rendered frames per second without speeding gameplay, native 1280x720 then 1920x1080 with expanded viewport and scalable UI, and improved pathfinding/basic AI while preserving difficulty and fog of war. Prioritize single player and current saves. Preserve community music, video and localization fixes. Only local Qwen Image 2.1 may generate/edit raster assets.

## Release gates

- Establish immutable executable/config/save baseline and reversible display presets.
- Measure actual frame updates independently of presentation. Movement/cast durations at 20/30 logic updates per second must differ by <=2%; validate 30 minutes of combat. Confirm 60 distinct rendered frames per second independently.
- Pathfinding: reachable goals reached, bounded unreachable searches, no illegal movement; compare static paths to an independent shortest-path oracle and test congestion recovery.
- User acceptance criterion (2026-09-27): the rerouting optimization succeeds only after safe mid-segment interruption is implemented. Preplanning and a handoff at the next cell alone do not satisfy it. Preserve position continuity, unit identity and valid occupancy, with native fallback for an unsafe transition.
- Native widescreen: expanded visible map, accurate picking/spell targeting, readable Chinese UI.
- Save/load, OGG/FMVs, alt-tab and rollback must remain functional.
